About the institute

Built around practical, guided learning

Dev Computer Institute provides computer education and practical training for learners who want to develop useful computer and digital skills. The institute offers courses covering computer fundamentals, data entry, DTP, Tally, web designing, Advanced Excel, and typing.

Our approach focuses on developing practical knowledge through structured courses, guided learning, and regular practice.

Mission

To provide practical and accessible computer education that helps learners develop useful skills and confidence.

Vision

To help learners build relevant computer skills for education, employment, and professional growth.

Practical, project-first

Courses are structured around building things, not just watching demonstrations.

Small, focused batches

Structured courses with guided practice; contact the institute for current batch details.

Clear skill progression

Each course is organized in stages, from fundamentals to applied practice.
